**Full description:**
OpenGymPR is a simple, fast gym log that does one thing well: tracking your lifts and your personal records.

LOG A SET IN SECONDS
Pick an exercise, tap + / − for weight and reps, hit Log set. It remembers your last weight and reps for every exercise.

SEE YOUR PRs THE MOMENT THEY HAPPEN
• Weight PR — heaviest ever
• e1RM PR — best estimated one-rep max (Epley)
• Rep PR — more reps at the same or heavier weight

PROGRESS AT A GLANCE
• e1RM chart per exercise (3 months, 1 year, all time)
• Rep-max table from 1RM to 12RM
• Full history, delete or undo any set

NO NONSENSE
• Free. No ads. No subscriptions.
• No account, no trackers, no analytics.
• No internet permission: your data cannot leave your phone.
• Export to JSON or CSV any time, import on a new phone.
• kg or lb, dark or light theme.
