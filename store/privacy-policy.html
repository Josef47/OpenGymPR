OpenGymPR - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-12

OpenGymPR does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal data.

  • No data collection. Everything you log (exercises, weights, reps, dates) is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • No internet access. The Android app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no third-party SDKs.
  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • Backups are under your control. If Android backup is enabled on your phone, Android may include the app's data in your own device backup (Google account / device-to-device transfer). You can also export a JSON or CSV file yourself; where that file goes is up to you.
  • Deleting your data: Settings → "Delete all data", or uninstall the app.

The source code is public (MIT licence), so anyone can verify these statements.
